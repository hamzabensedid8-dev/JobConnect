Contracts

Some contracts are free, some are paid

A placement is not always charged to the candidate. Each published contract states its own conditions before you apply — the price is always displayed, never hidden.

Free contracts0.00 — paid by the employer

The employer covers all recruitment costs. The candidate pays nothing: no application fee, no placement fee, no processing fee. The contract shows « No fee for the employee » and the amount due is 0.00. Free contracts are common for seasonal work, high-demand professions and internships arranged directly with the company.

Paid contractsJobConnect fee + recruiter price

For these contracts the candidate pays a single, non-recurring amount: the JobConnect service fee plus the price set by the recruiter. The exact total is shown before any payment, and the contract repeats it in section 4. Payment is made by Visa or Mastercard; a receipt is issued immediately.

Permanent employmentOpen-ended

An open-ended contract with the employer, subject to the trial period stated in the contract.

Fixed-term and temporary1 to 36 months

Contracts for a defined mission or season, renewable according to the labour code of the country.

Internships (stages)2 to 12 months

A training placement inside a company, with a supervisor, an educational objective and, where the law requires it, a grant. Internships may be free or paid depending on the host company.

Language stays (séjours linguistiques)2 weeks to 12 months

A language course abroad combined with accommodation, and optionally a work placement. The contract states the school, the number of hours per week, the accommodation and the total price.

Apprenticeship and freelanceVariable

Work-study programmes with alternating training, and independent contracts for consultants.

Legal

Terms, rules and conditions

These rules apply to every visitor, candidate and recruiter. They are written in plain English so that everyone can understand them before using the platform.

1. Purpose and acceptance

JobConnect is an online platform that connects candidates looking for work abroad with employers and recruiters. By creating an account, submitting a request or paying a fee, you accept these terms in full. If you do not accept them, you must not use the service.

JobConnect is not the employer. The employment relationship is created directly between the candidate and the employer named in the contract.

2. Accounts

You must give accurate information: real name, valid e-mail address, valid telephone number and genuine documents. One person, one account. You are responsible for keeping your password secret. Accounts used to publish false offers, to collect money without a contract, or to harass other users are suspended immediately and without compensation.

3. Requests and response times

Every request receives a reference code. The platform's target is to answer within 5 to 20 minutes during opening hours; outside those hours the answer comes at the start of the next session. A request is answered with one of three statuses: Pending, Accepted or Rejected. Every rejection states a reason, and that reason is visible with the reference code.

4. Free contracts and paid contracts

Some contracts are free: the employer pays all recruitment costs and the candidate owes nothing. Other contracts are paid: the candidate pays the JobConnect service fee plus the price set by the recruiter. The total amount is displayed before any payment and repeated in the contract. No candidate is ever asked to pay an amount that is not written in the contract.

Prices are shown in the currency of the contract. The JobConnect fee is a one-off service fee; it is not a salary advance, a deposit for a visa or a guarantee of employment.

5. Payment, receipts and refunds

Payments are handled by a card payment provider (Visa, Mastercard). JobConnect never receives or stores card numbers or security codes. A receipt is sent by e-mail within a few minutes after confirmation.

  • Before the contract is issued: if your request is rejected, the amount that is not attributable to work already performed is refunded to the same card within 14 days.
  • Duplicated payment: fully refunded.
  • After the contract is signed by both parties: the service has been delivered and the fee is no longer refundable, except where the law provides otherwise.
  • Fraudulent or unauthorised payment: the account is frozen and the case reported.
6. Code of conduct

Messages exchanged on the platform must remain professional. The following are strictly forbidden: asking a candidate for money outside the platform, promising a visa in exchange for payment, sending discriminatory or sexual content, impersonating an employer, and publishing an offer that does not correspond to a real position.

Messages can be deleted by the JobConnect team only, and a deletion applies to both parties. Any serious breach is reported to the competent authorities.

7. Responsibilities

JobConnect verifies the identity of employers and the conformity of contracts, but cannot guarantee that a visa will be granted or that an employer will respect the contract. The candidate is responsible for the accuracy of the documents provided. The employer is responsible for the salary, the working conditions and the legal formalities in the country of employment.

8. Personal data and privacy

We collect the information needed to process a request: name, contact details, nationality, resume and the details of the contract. This information is used only to provide the service, is stored on secured servers, and is never sold. It is kept as long as the account exists and, for accounting documents, for the period required by law. You may ask for access, correction or deletion of your data by writing to the contact address below.

9. Applicable law and disputes

These terms are governed by the law of the country where JobConnect is established. In the event of a dispute, the parties first seek an amicable solution. Failing agreement within thirty (30) days, the competent courts are seized. The employment contract itself is governed by the law of the country of employment.

10. Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated. The version published on this page is the one in force. Continuing to use the platform after a change means accepting the new version.
