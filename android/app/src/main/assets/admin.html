Change receiving account

Payments go to a merchant account (Stripe), never to a card number. Paste the keys of the account that should receive the money — the change applies immediately to new payments. The key is stored encrypted and only its last 4 characters are shown again.

Good to know

Visa and Mastercard rules forbid storing the 3-digit security code (CVC) of a card, so no platform can keep cards on file and switch between them the way you switch a phone number. Switching accounts with the keys above is the compliant equivalent: you point the platform to whichever of your merchant accounts should collect the money.
